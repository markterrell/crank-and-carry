Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Do wind-up radios work without mains electricity?

Many have a hand crank and/or an internal rechargeable battery, but features vary. Check the exact model instructions and do not assume every model has solar charging or phone charging.

Is DAB reception available everywhere?

No. Reception depends on location, building construction and the stations available. Check coverage and the retailer's return policy before choosing a DAB-only radio.
